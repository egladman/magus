# MGS3055: a spawn or fork above its parent's model tier

`magus job fork`, `magus\workspace().putJob` and the guard's spawn ceiling hold a
child to the rule the workspace was opened with (`magus.WithSpawnRule`). The CLI
passes `magus.TierCeiling`, which refuses a child whose tier ranks above its
parent's:

```text
[MGS3055] a job at model tier "routine" may not spawn one at "design":
  a child's tier is at most its parent's
```

The refusal is not retryable: the same fork fails the same way.

## What to do

- Fork the child at its parent's tier or lower (`--model <tier>`).
- If the work needs the higher tier, a person forks it as a row of its own.

A Go program that opens a workspace with a rule of its own matches this code with
`errors.Is(err, types.ModelTierAboveParent)` when its rule returns it.
