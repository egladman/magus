# MGS3038: a job postcondition no evidence could grade

`magus job fork`, `magus job apply` and `magus\workspace().putJob` refused a job record
because one of its postconditions could never be graded:

```text
[MGS3038] postcondition "names" expects the signature of 2 symbols, and a signature is one
  declaration: name one symbol per postcondition
```

Nothing was written to the job store. Every postcondition problem is reported at once,
each naming its field (`postconditions[1].symbols`, `postconditions[2].depends_on[0]`).

## What fires it

- A postcondition id that is not an id, or two postconditions sharing one.
- A kind outside `check`, `paths` and `symbol`, or an `expect` its kind does
  not accept.
- A postcondition carrying a subject its kind does not read, such as paths on a symbol
  postcondition, or naming nothing to examine at all.
- A `signature` on a postcondition that does not expect one, a signature postcondition naming
  other than one symbol, or two signature postconditions on one symbol.
- A `depends_on` entry naming no postcondition of the record, naming the postcondition itself, or
  closing a cycle.
- A symbol the knowledge graph resolves to more than one declaration.

## Why

A postcondition is what `magus job wait` grades the job by. A postcondition no evidence could
meet holds the job open forever, and one that names two subjects is graded on
whichever its kind happens to read.

## What to do

Edit each postcondition the problem names, using the fix it prints. For an ambiguous
symbol, name it by the id `magus refs <symbol>` prints.
