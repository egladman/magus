# MGS1076: a multi-line block shares its line

```text
{{hole}}
steps: {{steps:block}}
```

```text
x.yml.tmpl:2:8: [MGS1076] steps holds 6 lines and shares line 2; put the hole on its own line
```

## Why

A block carries the indentation before its hole onto every line after the first.
Text before or after the hole leaves no indentation to carry, so the lines after the
first would land at the wrong depth and break YAML or Python. A one-line block may
sit anywhere.

## Resolution

Put the hole on its own line, indented as its lines should be.
