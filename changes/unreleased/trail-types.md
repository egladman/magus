### Changed

- **Breaking: the guard trail record types drop the `Feedback` prefix.** In Go, package `types` renames `FeedbackTrail` to `Trail`, `FeedbackObservation` to `TrailObservation`, `FeedbackSpawn` to `TrailSpawn`, `FeedbackMark` to `TrailMark`, `FeedbackSection` to `TrailSection`, `FeedbackVerdict` to `TrailVerdict`, `FeedbackSections` to `TrailSections` and `FeedbackVerdicts` to `TrailVerdicts`.
