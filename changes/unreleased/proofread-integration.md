### Added

- **proofread flags three habits of generated writing in pull requests, commits and
  reviews.** `triad` flags series of exactly three, `stacked-colon` two clause colons in a
  paragraph, and `restates-diff` a bullet that only names a file and a verb. `review-length`
  advises on long review comments. All four ship off until a labeling round weighs them.

### Changed

- **proofread and the guard ask a pull request to lead with its intent.** lead-context wants
  what the change set out to do and why, and reads a `Cut:`, `Changed from plan:`,
  `Not done:` or `Not verified:` line as a stated limit. The guard's advice teaches that
  shape, and a denied reply asks whether you made the change.
- **proofread accepts "the doc says X, this does Y" as a reason** in `no-reason`, and
  `contrast` now judges review text.
