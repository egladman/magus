### Changed

- **A voice file sets the `triad` budget.** A text may hold as many series of three as the
  author's own 95th percentile allows at its length; the finding says when the voice set
  it.
- **Voice files are `proofread-voice/4`.** They keep the author's 5th and 95th percentile
  of each feature a budget or `uniform` reads. A `/3` file is refused; rerun
  `proofread voice build`.
