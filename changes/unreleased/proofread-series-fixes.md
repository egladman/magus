### Added

- **proofread's `triad` counts series of three verb phrases.** An item may run to six
  words, with or without the Oxford comma. `list-parallel` reads an irregular past verb
  (`built`, `wrote`) as past tense. A new `repeated-opener` rule (PRF1062) flags a list
  whose items mostly open on the same word; it ships off until a labeling round measures
  it.
