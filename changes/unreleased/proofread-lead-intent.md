### Changed

- **proofread asks a pull request's lead for the intent.** lead-context wants the first
  paragraph to say what the change set out to do and why, not what a reader can now do. A
  lead that opens on a defect passes when a purpose follows within two sentences. A `Cut:`,
  `Changed from plan:`, `Not done:` or `Not verified:` bullet or heading now counts as a
  stated limit.
