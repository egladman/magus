### Fixed

- **A leased script can commit in a repository of its own.** `vcs\cmd` under a lease may run `git commit` in a repository outside the job's checkout, unless its environment sets `GIT_DIR`, `GIT_WORK_TREE`, `GIT_COMMON_DIR` or `GIT_INDEX_FILE`. A case variant or firmlinked spelling of the checkout is the checkout. A write blocked by a declaration claim names the claim.
