### Added

- **A mandate holds a settled job too large to review in one sitting.** Past
  `--review-budget` lines in its scoped or full paths (400 by default, the same budget
  `split-into-branches.buzz` packs a layer to), the integration is held for the person,
  and the hold names the `split-into-branches.buzz --by symbols` command for that job. The
  mandate never splits a job itself.
