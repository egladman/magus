### Added

- **An author's note, `> [!AUTHOR]`, for the one place a page speaks as its author.**
  The docs site renders it as a dashed aside titled "Author's note", with a byline
  saying it is personal practice, not something magus promises.
- **proofread's `first-person` rule (PRF1005) reports I, me and my on a reference page
  or a guide outside an author's note.** It ships off; this repository denies it, with
  CONTRIBUTING.md, the design pages, the decision records and blog posts exempt.
