### Fixed

- **A spawn bound to a job row the hook's magus cannot read is denied at the spawn gate.**
  The denial names the root's remedy (rebuild the `./magus` the host hook runs, restart the
  daemon), so a skewed row never reaches a worker whose every call would be refused. A
  leased caller whose row has no checkout is told to stop and report an unresolved risk
  instead of a `<checkout>` placeholder. The `spawn-above-model-tier` advice now names
  `magus job apply` with a changed `model`.
