### Changed

- **The machine broker admits claims whose memory curves fit side by side.** A claim
  carries its measured memory curve, and the broker seats it when, at every future
  instant, it fits beside what is held, even when the peaks sum past the budget. A held
  claim that runs past its expected duration is charged its peak, and a claim with no
  curve still counts its peak for its whole life. A refusal says whether the broker
  packed by curve or by peak and names the instant the claims pass the budget.
