### Added

- **The agent guard judges an orchestrating session by its role.** The new
  `orchestrator-groundwork` rule refuses source reads and searches, full diffs, target runs
  (`-s` included), fetches and edits inside a checkout by a session magus recorded handing
  out work: a job row it forked that the store then showed live, or a spawn bound to one.
  Job verbs, graph reads and integration pass. A spawn bound to no row or a loaded
  magus-multi-agent skill only advises, and a session with no record, a worker, a subagent
  and a person are never judged. It is on in every workspace, the off level included.

### Changed

- **Breaking: `root-context-budget`, `root-heavy-command` and `root-repeat` only advise.**
  The strict level sets them to advise, and a workspace that declares `deny` for one gets
  MGS1045 naming `orchestrator-groundwork`. `root-heavy-command` and `root-repeat` skip a
  session that already hands out work. Command, write and spawn requests carry the
  caller's `Orchestration`.
