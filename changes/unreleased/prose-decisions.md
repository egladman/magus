### Added

- **Prose findings speak the guard's decisions and carry a `PRS` code.** Each finding names
  its rule, a code, `deny` or `advise`, and a link to `docs/reference/prose/<rule>/`; a
  decisions table sets any rule `off`, `advise` or `deny`. `judge-docs -catalog` lists every
  rule, and the docs render a page for each.
