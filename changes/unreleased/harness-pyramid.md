### Added

- **A design principle answers the common misreadings of "harness".** "The model proposes,
  the harness executes" takes seven misconceptions in turn, each with a cited source, then
  lays out the layers: the model, the inner harness (the host's agent loop), the outer
  harness magus supplies, and the person on top. Two figures draw the layers and two inner
  harnesses sharing one outer harness through job rows. The glossary gains entries for
  inner harness and outer harness.

### Changed

- **The scope page and the record of refusals call the agent loop an inner harness**, which
  matches the glossary: magus supplies a harness, the outer one, and never runs the loop.
