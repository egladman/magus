### Added

- **A design principle answers the common misreadings of "harness".** "The model proposes,
  the harness executes" takes seven misconceptions in turn, each with a cited source, then
  lays out the layers from the top: the person, who directs; the outer harness magus
  supplies, which serves them; the inner harness (the host's agent loop); and the model.
  Two figures draw the layers and two inner harnesses sharing one outer harness through job
  rows, with the person above both. The glossary gains entries for inner harness and outer
  harness.

### Changed

- **The scope page and the record of refusals call the agent loop an inner harness**, which
  matches the glossary: magus supplies a harness, the outer one, and never runs the loop.
