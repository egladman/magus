### Added

- **`magus diff` reports a sibling family.** The `sibling-sprawl` conformance check, on by default as advice, names a method or function joining siblings whose names differ in one complementary word, such as `RecordCacheHit` and `RecordCacheMiss`, weighed by shared parameters, the types carrying the family, and an owner past its `type-methods` value. Silent without a symbol index.
