# libs/marks

Marks drawn from geometry in Buzz, flat and two-color like buzzed's pull-tab icon.

```sh
magus run generate libs/marks    # every mark into gen/
```

`safetyscissors.buzz` draws the `libs/safetyscissors` mark: kids' safety scissors, half
open and leaning, each blade the hull of a heel and a round blunt nose. The pair is
balanced on the body's optical center, then nudged down and right where the eye reads
its weight. It comes in caution yellow and in blue, each as an app icon, a favicon and
a touch icon:

<p>
  <img src="gen/safetyscissors-yellow.svg" width="240" alt="Half-open safety scissors in charcoal on caution yellow.">
  <img src="gen/safetyscissors-blue.svg" width="240" alt="Half-open safety scissors in cream on blue.">
</p>

A `Design` sets the colors, how far the blades open, the nose radius and the tilt.
